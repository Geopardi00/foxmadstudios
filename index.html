Coming to Steam · 16 March 2027

A relaxing physics puzzle game. Bend gravity to invent postal routes between tiny planets, then watch parcels fly across the postal network you've built.

The jump lanes between the worlds went dark generations ago. Place attractors, repulsors and gates, slingshot cargo capsules past worlds and black holes, and grow your trade network until it reaches across the dark again.

  • ~100 hand-built routing puzzles
  • 6 regions, 40 stations
  • 8 languages

About the studio

I follow instinct, then sweat every detail.

FoxMad Studios is Matti, a solo developer in Finland. I make relaxed games with a clever twist: no stress and no rush, but real ideas to chew on. Half sharp fox, half mad idea: I chase strange concepts, then polish them until they feel effortless. My first game, Gravity Post, is built in Godot. More strange ideas are already brewing.
