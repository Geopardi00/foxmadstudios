Project Dyson's Swarm

A near-future space race played as a cargo puzzle. Pick your launch vehicle, pack every hold with just enough fuel and materials, and finish your moonbase before rival powers do. It's the first step toward a Dyson swarm.

Gravity Post (working title)

The jump lanes between worlds went dark generations ago. Bend gravity to engineer slingshot routes between tiny planets, earn stamps for every delivery and grow your postal network until it reaches across the dark again.

In development

Pesäpallo

Finland's own bat-and-ball game in stylised 3D. Pick your spot, choose your technique and time your swing against the vertical pitch, then race the throw around the zigzag bases. It's pesäpallo, not baseball with new names.
